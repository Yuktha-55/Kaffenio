A STORY IN EVERY CUP

Started with one
stubborn grinder.

We opened in a tiny room with one grinder, four chairs and a kettle that whistled off-key. People kept coming back, so we kept adding chairs.

Today the kettle still whistles, and we still learn your order by the second visit. We buy from small farms, roast in small batches and bake before sunrise.
